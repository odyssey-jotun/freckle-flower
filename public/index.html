Nikki, Freckle Flower

Twenty years of costumes, one very neglected garden.

I'm Nikki. I spent twenty years as a costume designer for theatre and cosplay, which means I've spent most of my adult life in a workroom full of people building something together, up to our elbows in fabric, losing track of time. I know what that room does for a person. I also know what happens when life crowds it out. My own garden could tell you.

I'm also the planner in my family. Airbnbs for the trips, venues for the parties, the whole itinerary. Somewhere along the way I noticed that planning an event is a lot like planning a play. It's a full production. There are a hundred moving parts and deadlines that have to be met, even when they aren't. Opening night doesn't move. Theatre taught me how to get everything ready anyway.

So that's my job here. I run the production. You show up and do the thing you love.
